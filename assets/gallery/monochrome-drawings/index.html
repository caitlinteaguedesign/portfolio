Monochrome Drawings


This section contains select work from my undergraduate drawing classes, specifically all works in black and white. Technically, if you're artistically or linguistically astute, you know that monochrome does not have to mean just black and white, it can mean shades and tints of any one hue on the color wheel. My word choice is more about convenience and style preference in my file naming structure, so for our purposes these are the drawings absent of color.

I took three drawing courses at my first college, and then was advised to repeat the last drawing class at the university I would later graduate from. I spoke well when asked about my portfolio, but many of my works across 3D and 2D were “found wanting”. [4] Suffice to say I needed to hone my skills more, so I retook Drawing 102 and it helped a lot. Here's a demonstration of that skill growth.
