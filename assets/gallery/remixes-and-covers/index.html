Remixes and Covers


And now for something completely different: things I've made that are not entirely of my sole authorship. In these works I have taken something or pieces of several somethings that someone else has created and I put my own spin on it. I like these music terms (when no music is present) as helpful analogies. Just like musicians, in my mediums my work can sometimes be derivative. (I like to joke that all art is derivative, but by that I don't mean no art has value). In fact, some of my favorite songs use samples of older works [6], and some songs I heard a cover in a genre different from the original long before I heard the original. [7]

What follows is a mixture of physical media I hand-crafted and digital experiences whose designs are based on physical media.
