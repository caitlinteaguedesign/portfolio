Gallery IV
Paintings


I took oil painting and watercolor courses at my first college. I continued to demonstrate some skills in color theory but composition and articulation were not in my list of strengths. For these reasons, some works presented here are actually partials of larger works.

At my last university, my courses began to veer into computer and vector work, and I had little reason to return to physical tools. In this collection, I am choosing to bend definitions and include vector work that reminds me of paintings. Naturally, I've included digital paintings as well.
