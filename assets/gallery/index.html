un art [1]

My conceit for this site is twofold: one, I am an artist, and two, I don't have to be too serious about it. The “too serious” in the last sentence is doing some heavy lifting, because on the one-hand, I should take myself seriously as an artist (that I love what I've made and want share it widely without care of critique), but my way of showing up, at times, comes with a few spoonfuls of self-deprecating humor.

In other places you'll find interactive experiences I've coded. This gallery is for the things that exist, or could exist, on physical media in the real world, in some form. The experience begins when the viewer beholds or holds the creation, and ends when they put it down and walk away from it. Here's a digital version of that experience.

While I make this website and the self-doubt creeps in, I turn to things that give me joy and comfort. I should not feel embarrassment trying to take up space, for this is The Internet, and as the song goes: [2]

Welcome to the internet, have a look around.
Anything that brain of yours can think of can be found.

We've got mountains of content, some better, some worse.
If none of it's of interest to you, you'd be the first.

May a non-zero number of people discover and enjoy my little corner of the internet. [3]
