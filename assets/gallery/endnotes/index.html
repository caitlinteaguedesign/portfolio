Appendix A
Endnotes


I have lots of thoughts all of the time, so rather than ramble on I've added endnotes throughout the gallery. This is the space for folks who like to get curious and follow brain droppings, or for folks who have an incredible amount of idle time on their hands. You can return to your spot with the back button, or if you're lost your way entirely head back to the gallery entrance and begin your journey again. Happy trails!

  1. “An art” is a running joke in the blog, “McMansion Hell”. At some point in repeating this reference with my husband the “an” transformed into the Spanish version of the article, “un”. To explain further would run us into “if you have to explain the joke too much”, it probably is not that funny. We think we're funny to each other. 😅

  2. “Welcome to the Internet" by Bo Burnham, from the album and Netflix special INSIDE. It was the first music-comedy special I enjoyed (I previously thought music-dash-comedy acts were kind of cheesy, but this one hit me at the right time). Bo's got some bops, and it helped that we being close in age meant his cultural references and experiences were relatable to mine.

  3. Every time I invoke the phrase, “a non-zero number of people”, I'm reminded of a web developer friend of mine who has been stalwart advocate for accessibility and progressive enhancement on the web. You can check out Matei Stanca's work over at Ambient.Impact.

    Return to homepage
  4. The phrase “found wanting” comes from the movie A Knight's Tale. It is a highly quotable film (at least, by me and my sister), and an interesting one because of creative choices the production team decided on. They purposefully add modern elements to a medievally-set story, like opening with a jousting tournament set to Queen's “We Will Rock You” while the spectators pound out the beat and shout along. Historically accurate no, but meeting the audience where they are at, highly effective at storytelling.

    Return to Monochrome Drawings
  5. Look, Minecraft might seem like just a kids' game today, but back in my day (beta version, Java edition) it was a game primarily played by early 20-somethings. I got into it (after initially passing it up for it's low quality graphics) after I started watching The Yogscast (around 2010). I liked watched Simon's and Lewis' antics, and it endeared me to giving the game try. Years later, the Minecraft-mood will hit me every now and then, and I'll make a new world and abandon it after creating a new base. On rarer occasions I peer pressure my husband into playing with me. He's my cave delver while I stay behind to do my favorite part, "homesteading" (base design and supplies organization).

    Return to Sketches
  6. One example: "Stronger" by Kanye West*. This song was a banger when I first heard it, in no small part because it samples "Harder, Better, Faster, Stronger" by Daft Punk. The music video has moments of sampling with the art direction, by taking inspiration from the anime films Akira and Interstella 5555. Two actors dressed as Daft Punk are also featured in the music video.

    *Kanye, or Ye, has at times made deplorable comments and inappropriate music. Visitors are advised in case they'd like to skip any links to his work.
